# SEO audit: subtitlemate.com (2026-10-01)

Audited the local build (`npm run build`, 12 pages). subtitlemate.com does not resolve yet, so nothing live was checked: no Core Web Vitals field data, no headers, no GSC data.

**Score: 59 / 100.** Most of the loss is two placeholders and a few missing files. The tool pages themselves are in good shape.

## Critical: fix before submitting to GSC

**Status: 1 to 3 and 4 (rename to SubtitleMate) fixed 2026-10-01.** Build is `format: 'file'` for Cloudflare; `gaId` is still a placeholder.

1. **Every canonical and sitemap URL is `https://example.com/...`.** `src/site.ts` still has `url: 'https://example.com'`. Google will treat example.com as the real site and ignore yours. Set it to `https://subtitlemate.com`. Same file: `email` and `gaId` are placeholders too.
2. **No `robots.txt`.** Add `public/robots.txt` with `Sitemap: https://subtitlemate.com/sitemap.xml`. GSC works without it, but it's how other crawlers find the sitemap.
3. **Canonical URLs have no trailing slash, but pages are built as `/tool/index.html`.** On Cloudflare Pages, Netlify and GitHub Pages, `/srt-cleaner` redirects to `/srt-cleaner/`. Then every canonical points at a redirect and GSC reports "Page with redirect". Fix one of two ways: `build: { format: 'file' }` in `astro.config.mjs` (serves `/srt-cleaner`, matches current canonicals), or `trailingSlash: 'always'` and add the slash in `Layout.astro` and `sitemap.xml.ts`. Check after deploy: `curl -I https://subtitlemate.com/srt-cleaner` should return 200, not 301/308.

## High

4. **Brand mismatch.** The domain is SubtitleMate, but every title, the header logo and the legal pages say "Subtitle Tools". That's a generic phrase Google can't tie to you, and nobody searching for "subtitlemate" will match it. Rename `site.name` and the hardcoded "Subtitle Tools" text.
5. **Dead "Get premium" and "Login" links (`href="#"`) on every page** (`src/components/Header.astro:31,43`). They promise features that don't exist. That hurts trust (Google's quality raters look for this) and they're wasted links in the nav. Remove until they're real.
6. **No favicon.** Google shows the favicon next to your result. Without one you get a generic globe. Add `public/favicon.svg` (and a 48x48+ PNG/ICO) and link it in `Layout.astro`.
7. **No `og:image`.** Shared links on social and chat apps show no picture. One 1200x630 image for the whole site is enough to start.
8. **Titles too long on 7 of 8 tool pages (69 to 82 chars).** Google cuts at about 60. The " | Subtitle Tools" suffix is what gets cut, plus part of the keyword phrase. Shorten, e.g. `SRT Cleaner: Remove Tags and SDH | SubtitleMate`.

## Medium

9. **No site-level schema.** Add `WebSite` + `Organization` on the homepage, and `WebApplication` (name, `applicationCategory: "MultimediaApplication"`, `offers` price 0, `operatingSystem: "Any"`) on each tool page. This helps Google understand they're free tools.
10. **FAQPage schema won't show rich results.** Since 2023 Google only shows FAQ rich results for government and health sites. Keep it (it doesn't hurt, and AI tools read it), but don't expect FAQ dropdowns in search.
11. **Meta descriptions over 160 chars on 4 pages** (partial shifter 178, validator 177, UTF-8 182, convert-to-SRT 165). End gets cut. Trim to about 150.
12. **Homepage is thin: 174 words.** It's a list of tool links. Add a short paragraph on what the site does, which formats it handles (SRT, WebVTT, ASS, SAMI, MicroDVD, MPL2), and why "never uploaded" matters.
13. **No About page.** `docs/seo/SITE-STRUCTURE.md` plans `/about`. A short page on who built it and why helps trust.
14. **H1s are just the tool name** ("SRT cleaner"). Fine, but adding the job helps: "SRT cleaner: remove tags and SDH from subtitles".

## Low

15. `sitemap.xml` has no `<lastmod>`. Google uses it to decide when to recrawl. Optional.
16. Privacy, terms and contact are in the sitemap. Fine to keep; they won't rank and don't need to.
17. No `llms.txt`. Google ignores it; some AI tools read it. Skip unless you want it.

## What already works

- Static HTML, fast, no render-blocking JS. The 340 KB worker loads only when a file is picked.
- Every page has a unique title, description, one H1, `lang="en"`, viewport meta, canonical tag.
- Tool pages have 450 to 570 words of real help text and FAQ.
- Sitemap is generated from the pages, so new tools get added automatically.
- Every page links to every tool (header menu + related tools).
- GA4 only loads after consent.

## Connecting GSC

1. Fix items 1 to 3, deploy.
2. In GSC add a **Domain property** for `subtitlemate.com` and verify with the DNS TXT record at your registrar. That covers http/https and www/non-www, and needs no code change.
3. Pick one host (with or without `www`) and redirect the other to it at the host/DNS level.
4. Sitemaps → submit `https://subtitlemate.com/sitemap.xml`.
5. URL Inspection → test the homepage and one tool page → Request indexing.
